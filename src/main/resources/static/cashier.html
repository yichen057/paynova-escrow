⚠️ Illustration only: reconstructed client-side from the operations this page performed — the authoritative escrow ledger lives in PostgreSQL (ledger_entries of system:escrow; see the psql query in the README). Notice it mirrors the wallets: every buyer DEBIT on fund appears here as a CREDIT, and vice versa.
Convention — relative to this account: CREDIT = money IN (+)  ·  DEBIT = money OUT (−). Ledger amounts are always positive; the direction column carries the sign. Every transaction has a matching opposite entry on another account — that's double-entry.
Escrow lifecycle: CREATED → FUNDED → RELEASED / REFUNDED. Behind every button: JWT auth, idempotency records, a double-entry ledger posting, pessimistic account locks, and an outbox event.
